How private events work

We’ve hosted Pilates parties, bachelorettes, and birthday events — they’ve been so special. Here’s the format most people book, plus a few details to keep in mind.

The usual format

A fun hour with an instructor, plus time to decorate and celebrate. About 2 hours of studio time.

  1. 30 min

    Before

    Setup and decorate — you’ll have the room before class starts.

  2. 1 hour

    Workout

    A fun class with one of our instructors.

  3. 30 min

    After

    Pictures, mingling, cake, and enjoying the moment.

That’s our most popular format. We’re happy to create a custom package if you have something else in mind.

The package

$550

Last class of the day, or a morning slot — so it doesn’t interrupt the regular schedule.

  • 30 minutes before for setup and decoration
  • 1-hour workout with an instructor
  • 30 minutes after for photos, mingling, and celebrating
  • A table for cake and treats if you bring them
  • Our team is happy to help capture memories

How payment works

After you inquire, we’ll check that the date is available and email you about reserving it. The date is held once the deposit is in. The rest is due the day before the event.

  1. To reserve

    $200

    Deposit to hold your date and time.

  2. Day before

    $350

    Remaining balance if the package is $550 with no add-ons. Styling or extra time is added here if you booked it.

Example: Reformer package + styling is $700 total — $200 to reserve, $500 the day before.

A few things to keep in mind

Extra time

To respect our instructors and daily schedule, extra time is $50 for every 30 minutes beyond the agreed time — including late arrivals.

Stay in your room

If other classes are running at the same time, the event stays in the room you booked. If nothing else is happening in parallel, you’re welcome to use the lobby too.
