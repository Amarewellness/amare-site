Delete your AMARÉ app account

Information for AMARÉ mobile app users

Last updated August 25, 2026

This page explains how to delete your AMARÉ mobile app account. It applies to the native AMARÉ app for iOS and Android. It does not delete your studio client profile, memberships, or billing by itself.

Delete in the app (recommended)

If you can open the AMARÉ app and sign in:

  1. Open the app and sign in with your email one-time passcode.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete AMARÉ app account.
  3. Tap Send verification code and enter the code sent to your sign-in email.
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted.

Need help with billing, membership, or bookings instead of deleting your app account? Use the in-app Contact screen or email us (see below).

Cannot access the app?

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app installed, email info@amarewellness.com from the same email address you use for AMARÉ. Ask us to delete your AMARÉ app account. We may verify your identity before completing the request.

What app account deletion does

  • Removes your AMARÉ app sign-in and signs you out of the app.
  • Revokes active AMARÉ app sessions and mobile access tokens.
  • Deletes push notification tokens and in-app notification preferences tied to that account.
  • Unlinks your AMARÉ identity from the studio profile association used for app access.

What it does not do

Important: Deleting your AMARÉ app account does not cancel memberships, billing, class credits, or upcoming bookings.

Studio and Mindbody records (for example, your studio client profile, visit history, or class reservations) may be retained according to studio and Mindbody policies. Stripe or other payment records related to past purchases may also be retained for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

To change billing, membership, or bookings, contact the studio at info@amarewellness.com or visit our Contact page.
