Shipping Policy

Last updated April 29, 2026

This policy applies to retail merchandise orders (for example grip socks and other physical products) that we ship to you. Class packages, memberships, and studio services are not covered here.

We currently ship within the United States.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

Shipping time

Standard delivery typically takes 3–5 business days depending on the destination.

Shipping rates

  • Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.
  • Orders under $50 are shipped at a flat rate of $6.99.

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information.
