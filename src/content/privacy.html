Privacy Policy

A legal disclaimer

Last updated August 25, 2026

At AMARÉ Wellness Studio, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details (name, email address, phone number) when you fill out forms or book sessions.
  • Payment information for service transactions.
  • Website usage data via cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage bookings and services.
  • Communicate with you about your sessions, promotions, or updates.
  • Improve our website and services.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party providers (for example, booking or payment systems) who help us operate our business, under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Your data rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of promotional emails at any time.
  • Request information about how your data is used.

5. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can modify your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.

7. AMARÉ mobile app account deletion

If you use the AMARÉ mobile app, you can delete your AMARÉ app account from Profile → Delete AMARÉ app account. For your security, we ask you to re-verify with a one-time code sent to your sign-in email before deletion is completed.

When you delete your AMARÉ app account, we:

  • Delete or deactivate your AMARÉ app account and remove AMARÉ app sign-in access.
  • Revoke active AMARÉ app sessions and mobile access tokens.
  • Delete push notification tokens and in-app notification preferences tied to that account.
  • Unlink your AMARÉ identity from the studio profile association used for app access.

Important: Deleting your AMARÉ app account does not cancel memberships, billing, class credits, or upcoming bookings. Mindbody studio records (for example, your studio client profile, visit history, or class reservations managed by the studio) may be retained according to studio and Mindbody policies. Stripe or other payment records related to past purchases may also be retained for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes.

To change billing, membership, or bookings, please contact the studio directly at info@amarewellness.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
