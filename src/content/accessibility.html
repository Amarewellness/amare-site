Accessibility Statement

This statement was last updated on March 15, 2026.

We at AMARÉ Wellness Studio are committed to making our website accessible to people with disabilities. Our goal is to ensure that all visitors can browse the site easily and enjoy the same level of experience regardless of ability.

What web accessibility is

An accessible website allows visitors with disabilities to browse the site with the same or a similar level of ease and enjoyment as other visitors. This can be achieved through the capabilities of the system on which the site is operating and through assistive technologies such as screen readers and keyboard navigation.

Accessibility adjustments on this site

This website has been designed in accordance with the WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines and aims to meet level AA accessibility standards.

As part of our efforts to improve accessibility, we have implemented the following adjustments:

  • Ongoing review of navigation, forms, and interactive elements for common barriers.
  • Set the primary language of the website.
  • Defined clear heading structures across pages.
  • Added alternative text to images where appropriate.
  • Ensured color contrast between text and background for readability.
  • Reduced unnecessary motion and animations where possible.
  • Designed the website to support keyboard navigation and screen readers.

Third-party content

Some pages may contain elements provided by third-party services such as embedded social media content or booking platforms. These elements are managed by external providers and may not fully comply with accessibility standards.
