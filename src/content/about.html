About us

AMARÉ Wellness Studio is Hallandale’s home for intentional movement—a community where Reformers, mat work, Kangoo Jumps, barre, and more live under one thoughtful roof. We designed the space around how we teach: natural light, modern equipment, warm wood and soft neutrals, so the room feels calm the moment you arrive and you can focus on your body, not the noise around you.

Our team believes movement should feel welcoming, not intimidating. Whether you’re new to Pilates or building a steady practice, you’ll find clear coaching, careful attention to form, and a studio culture that remembers your name.

Atmosphere

We design experiences—not just classes. From big Reformer days to add-ons like sound and celebration styling, we layer lighting, music, and detail so the room feels as intentional as the programming.
