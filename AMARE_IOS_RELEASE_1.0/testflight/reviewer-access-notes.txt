TestFlight / App Review — reviewer access notes
=================================================
App: AMARÉ Wellness (com.amarewellness.app)

Same reviewer flow as Google Play (see AMARE_ANDROID_RELEASE_1.0/play-console-drafts/review-notes.txt).

SIGN-IN FOR REVIEWERS
---------------------
• Email OTP only — no password, no Google, no Apple login buttons
• Reviewers may use ANY email address they can access
• No demo account or OTP bypass required
• If OTP is delayed: info@amarewellness.com

STEPS FOR APP REVIEW
--------------------
1. Open app — browse Schedule without signing in
2. (Optional) Sign in:
   a. Tap Sign in
   b. Enter any accessible email
   c. Enter OTP from inbox
   d. If new user: first name, last name, phone → continue
3. (Optional) Browse Profile, My Classes
4. Do NOT require real booking unless Apple specifically needs it
5. Do NOT require real payment — Stripe opens in Safari; no card entry in app

PUBLIC ACCESS
-------------
Schedule is visible without login. Reviewers can evaluate core functionality
without creating an account.

BOOKING WARNING
---------------
Signed-in booking may create a REAL studio reservation in Mindbody.
Review notes instruct reviewers to skip unless necessary.

PAYMENTS
--------
Stripe Hosted Checkout in system browser (Safari).
Physical in-studio Pilates services — not digital IAP.
No real payment required for review.

ACCOUNT DELETION
----------------
Profile → Delete AMARÉ app account → email OTP
Public: https://www.amarewellness.com/mobile-account-deletion

PUSH
----
Optional. Profile → Notifications to enable.
If push disabled in submitted build, note that in App Review reply.

RECOMMENDED REVIEWER MAILBOX (studio setup — optional)
------------------------------------------------------
Create a monitored inbox for review periods, e.g.:
  apple-review@amarewellness.com  (suggested — not yet confirmed)

Same pattern as Google Play reviewer email setup.
Ensure OTP delivery works to that inbox before submission.

TESTFLIGHT INTERNAL TESTERS
---------------------------
Before external beta:
  - Add studio team Apple IDs as Internal Testers in App Store Connect
  - Run testflight-qa-checklist.txt on real iPhone
  - Fix iOS-only issues before submitting to App Review

APP STORE CONNECT — APP REVIEW INFORMATION
------------------------------------------
Demo account: Not required (OTP with any email)
Notes field: Paste app-store-metadata/review-notes.txt
Contact: info@amarewellness.com + support URL
