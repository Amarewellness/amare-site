AMARÉ Wellness is the companion app for AMARÉ Wellness Studio in Hallandale Beach, FL.

Browse the class schedule, book and manage Pilates classes, view your upcoming visits, track memberships and class packages, and stay connected with important studio updates.

With the AMARÉ app, you can:
• Browse the public class schedule
• Book and manage Pilates classes
• View your next class details
• Track your membership or class credits
• Get directions, parking, and first-visit information
• Bring a friend to class
• Receive optional class reminders and booking updates

Sign in with a one-time email code. No password required.

Designed for AMARÉ members and guests. The app helps you plan your next visit and manage your studio experience from your phone.
